🔊 Zumerograd Audio (v1.0)
Zumerograd Audio is the ultimate tool for total sound destruction. Forget about "high-fidelity" or "clean" audio. We’re here to redline your bass, crush your bitrates, and transform any track into an underground SoundCloud masterpiece.

✨ Key Features
Extreme Bass Boost: Pump the low-end until your speakers literally want to leave the chat.

Halal Injection: Randomly injects audio clips from your sounds folder. Perfect for memes, tags, and unexpected drops.

Glitch & Bitcrush: Digital signal destruction effects for that raw, "broken" underground texture.

Abyssal Echo: Deep reverberation that makes your music sound like it’s playing in a massive, empty concrete bunker.

Cyberpunk GUI: A sleek PyQt6 interface inspired by retro hacker terminals and military oscilloscopes.

🛠 Setup & Launch

Install the archive, unzip it, open the terminal and type:
py C:\Users\"username"\Downloads\ZumerogradAudio\ZumerogradAudio.py

Also don't forget to install dependencies:
py -m pip install PyQt6 pydub numpy

If your antivirus complains about a Trojan, click "Allow on device." This is a safe file.

🎮 How to Use
Select Audio: Choose your source track (Macan, Phonk, or whatever needs "improving").

Bass Level: The power slider. Warning: May cause structural damage to your neighbors' walls.

Effect Freq: Controls glitch intensity and how often random sounds from the sounds folder are injected.

START: Hit the button and wait for the "DONE" message. Your masterpiece will appear in the sounds folder with the halalGUI_ prefix.
